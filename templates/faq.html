Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

What is Divine Bridge?
Divine Bridge is a prayer platform that connects believers from around the world. We provide a space for sharing prayer requests, accessing daily prayers, and building a supportive spiritual community.
Is Divine Bridge free to use?
Yes, Divine Bridge is completely free to use. We believe in making spiritual resources accessible to everyone.
How do I create an account?
Creating an account is easy! Simply click the "Sign Up" button in the top right corner, fill out the registration form with your name and email, and you'll be ready to start using Divine Bridge.

Prayer Requests

How do I request prayer?
To request prayer, log in to your account, click on "Request Prayer" in the navigation menu, fill out the form with your request details, and click "Submit". Your request will be shared with our prayer community.
Are prayer requests private?
You can choose to make your prayer request public or private. Private requests are only visible to our prayer team, while public requests can be seen and prayed for by the entire community.
How do I pray for others?
You can pray for others by browsing the public prayer requests on our platform. When you find a request you'd like to pray for, you can click the "I'm Praying" button to show your support and add it to your prayer list.

Account & Privacy

How do I update my account information?
You can update your account information by going to your profile settings. Click on your profile picture in the top right corner, select "Settings", and you'll be able to modify your information.
How is my personal information protected?
We take your privacy seriously. Your personal information is encrypted and stored securely. We never share your information with third parties without your consent. For more details, please read our Privacy Policy.
How do I delete my account?
To delete your account, go to your profile settings, scroll to the bottom, and click on "Delete Account". Please note that this action is permanent and cannot be undone.
