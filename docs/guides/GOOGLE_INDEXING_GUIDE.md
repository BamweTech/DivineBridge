## STEP 1: Verify with Google Search Console (CRITICAL)

### Option A: HTML File Verification (Recommended - Fastest)

1. **Go to Google Search Console**
   - Visit: https://search.google.com/search-console
   - Sign in with your Google account

2. **Add Property**
   - Click "Add Property"
   - Select "URL prefix"
   - Enter: `https://divinebridge.io`
   - Click "Continue"

3. **Choose HTML File Verification**
   - Google will give you a file like: `google1234567890abcdef.html`
   - **Download this file**

4. **Upload to Divine Bridge**
   - Save the file to: `/static/google1234567890abcdef.html`
   - The route is already set up to serve it automatically
   - Deploy the change

5. **Verify**
   - Click "Verify" in Google Search Console
   - ✅ You're now verified!

### Option B: DNS Verification (Long-term)

1. In Google Search Console, choose "DNS record"
2. Copy the TXT record they provide
3. Add to your domain's DNS settings:
   ```
   Type: TXT
   Name: @
   Value: google-site-verification=XXXXX
   ```
4. Wait 10-60 minutes for DNS propagation
5. Click "Verify"

## STEP 2: Submit Your Sitemap

**Once verified:**

1. In Google Search Console, go to "Sitemaps" (left sidebar)
2. Click "Add new sitemap"
3. Enter: `sitemap.xml`
4. Click "Submit"

**Google will now:**
- ✅ Crawl your sitemap within 24 hours
- ✅ Discover all your pages automatically
- ✅ Index disaster pages, prayer pages, etc.
