Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

What is Divine Bridge?
Divine Bridge is a prayer platform that connects believers from around the world. We provide a space for sharing prayer requests, accessing daily prayers, and building a supportive spiritual community.
Is Divine Bridge free to use?
Yes, Divine Bridge is completely free to use. We believe in making spiritual resources accessible to everyone.
How do I create an account?
Creating an account is easy! Simply click the "Sign Up" button in the top right corner, fill out the registration form with your name and email, and you'll be ready to start using Divine Bridge.

Prayer Requests

How do I request prayer?
To request prayer, log in to your account, click on "Request Prayer" in the navigation menu, fill out the form with your request details, and click "Submit". Your request will be shared with our prayer community.
Are prayer requests private?
You can choose to make your prayer request public or private. Private requests are only visible to our prayer team, while public requests can be seen and prayed for by the entire community.
How do I pray for others?
You can pray for others by browsing the public prayer requests on our platform. When you find a request you'd like to pray for, you can click the "I'm Praying" button to show your support and add it to your prayer list.

Daily Prayer & Verses

What is the daily global prayer?
Our daily global prayer is a comprehensive prayer generated each day at midnight UTC that addresses current world crises, natural disasters, conflicts, and humanitarian needs. It's researched from the latest news (last 72 hours) and prays specifically for affected regions and people worldwide.
How do I get today's Bible verse?
Visit our homepage or Prayer Room to see today's inspiring Bible verse. Each day features a new verse chosen to provide encouragement and spiritual guidance. You can also download beautiful verse images to share on social media.
Can I download and share prayer images?
Yes! Each daily prayer and verse comes with a beautifully designed image optimized for social media (Instagram, Facebook, Twitter). Simply click the "Download Image" button on the daily prayer or verse page. On mobile, you can save directly to your photos or share instantly.
What time is the daily prayer released?
The daily prayer is automatically generated and released at midnight UTC (Coordinated Universal Time) every day. This ensures believers worldwide have access to fresh, relevant prayer content each morning.

Disaster & Crisis Support

How can I pray for disaster victims?
You can pray for disaster victims in several ways: (1) Join our daily global prayer which covers recent disasters worldwide, (2) Visit disaster-specific prayer pages like /prayers/disaster/flood/mexico for targeted prayers, (3) Submit your own prayer request if you're affected by or concerned about a specific disaster, (4) Share our prayer images on social media to unite more people in prayer.
What disasters are covered in today's prayer?
Today's prayer covers all major disasters and crises from the last 72 hours, including natural disasters (earthquakes, floods, hurricanes, wildfires), armed conflicts, disease outbreaks, and humanitarian crises. View today's prayer to see specific locations and situations being prayed for.
Are there prayers for specific disasters?
Yes! We automatically create targeted prayer pages for each disaster mentioned in our daily prayers. These pages provide specific prayers for that disaster and location (e.g., "Prayer for People Affected by Flood in Mexico"). You can find these through our sitemap or by searching for the disaster on our site.
How do I find prayers for my location?
If your area is affected by a disaster mentioned in our daily prayer, a dedicated prayer page will be automatically created at /prayers/disaster/[disaster-type]/[location]. You can also submit a personal prayer request specific to your situation in the Prayer Room.
Can I submit a prayer request for a disaster in my area?
Absolutely! Click "Request Prayer" in the Prayer Room and share your specific situation. Whether you're directly affected by a disaster or concerned about your community, our global prayer community is here to pray with you and support you.
How often are disaster prayers updated?
Our daily global prayer is updated every 24 hours with the latest disaster information. Disaster-specific prayer pages are generated when a crisis is mentioned in the daily prayer and remain accessible for ongoing support and prayer.
Do you pray for wars and conflicts?
Yes, our daily prayer includes prayers for people affected by armed conflicts, wars, and violence worldwide. We pray for peace, protection for civilians, wisdom for leaders, and healing for communities torn apart by conflict.
Can churches use Divine Bridge for their prayer ministry?
Yes! Churches are welcome to use our daily prayers, Bible verses, and images for their services, small groups, and prayer meetings. You can share our content freely, and we encourage church communities to join our global prayer network.

Account & Privacy

How do I update my account information?
You can update your account information by going to your profile settings. Click on your profile picture in the top right corner, select "Settings", and you'll be able to modify your information.
How is my personal information protected?
We take your privacy seriously. Your personal information is encrypted and stored securely. We never share your information with third parties without your consent. For more details, please read our Privacy Policy.
How do I delete my account?
To delete your account, go to your profile settings, scroll to the bottom, and click on "Delete Account". Please note that this action is permanent and cannot be undone.
